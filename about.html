About Threadly

Our Story

Threadly started in 2020 with a simple idea: great clothing shouldn't be expensive or complicated. What began as a small team of friends in Dehradun is now a growing brand dressing thousands of people across India.

We pick soft fabrics, fair prices and timeless designs, so you can look good and feel comfortable from morning to night.

Every order is checked by hand before it leaves our warehouse – because details matter.
